## 1. Overview

Harp2Tab is a harmonica tab generator, available as an Android app and as a web app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights as a user. We collect as little data as possible, and both versions are designed to work on your own device.

**Accounts are optional, and are currently available only in the web app.** You can record, edit and export tabs without ever creating one. The Android app has no accounts at all — nothing in section 2's "Account Information" applies to it.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### Microphone Audio
- **What:** The app accesses your microphone to detect the pitch of notes you play.
- **How it is used:** Audio is processed in real time on your device using a pitch detection algorithm. It is never recorded to a file, never stored, and never transmitted to any server.
- **Retention:** Zero — audio data is discarded immediately after processing each note.

### Tab Recordings
- **What:** The harmonica tabs you generate (hole numbers and directions) are stored locally on your device.
- **How it is used:** To let you review, edit, and export your recordings within the app.
- **Retention:** Until you delete them within the app or uninstall the app. Tab data is included in Android's automatic backup to your personal Google Drive account — this backup is controlled by you and your Google account settings.

### Purchase Information
- **What:** Whether you have purchased the full version of the app.
- **How it is used:** To unlock unlimited recordings and export features.
- **How it is handled:** Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive, store, or process any payment card details. Purchase status is stored locally on your device and backed up via Android Auto Backup.

### Account Information (web app only, and only if you create an account)

- **What:** Your email address, the sign-in methods you use (Google, or email and password), a randomly generated account identifier, a display name if you choose to set one, and the dates your account was created and last used. If you sign in with Google, Google supplies your email address and name; Google also supplies a link to your profile picture, which is stored on the account record but is not shown or used anywhere in the app — Harp2Tab displays your initials instead.
- **How it is used:** Solely to identify your account so you can sign back in, and — once the feature is available — to keep your tabs in step across your devices.
- **Where it is held:** Firebase Authentication (operated by Google LLC).
- **What we never receive:** your Google password, or any other credential. Passwords you set for a Harp2Tab account are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication; we cannot see them.
- **Retention:** Until you delete your account, which you can do yourself at any time from the Profile page in the web app. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

### Your Tabs and an Account

**At present, creating an account does not upload your tabs.** Your tabs stay on the device that made them, exactly as described above, and the only thing stored on our side is the account record itself. Cross-device sync is being built; when it is released this policy will be updated before it is switched on, and the "Last updated" date will change.

### Crash Reports
- **What:** If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics (operated by Google LLC).
- **What the report contains:** Device model, Android OS version, app version, and the technical crash trace. It does not contain your name, email, microphone audio, or tab content.
- **How it is used:** Solely to identify and fix bugs.
- **Retention:** 90 days in Firebase Crashlytics, per Google's default retention policy.

## 3. Data We Do NOT Collect

- Location data
- Contacts, photos, or any other device data
- Advertising identifiers
- Usage analytics or behavioural tracking
- Payment card details (see Purchase Information above)
- Microphone audio, which is never stored or transmitted (see above)

**Names and email addresses:** we hold these only for web-app accounts, and only if you choose to create one — see "Account Information" above. If you do not create an account, we hold none of it. The Android app collects neither under any circumstances.

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

- **Access:** Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you. If you have a web-app account, everything we hold is shown on your Profile page.
- **Deletion:** If you have an account, you can delete it yourself from the Profile page in the web app; this removes your account record permanently and immediately. If you do not have an account, we hold nothing on our servers, and uninstalling the app (or clearing your browser's site data) removes everything stored locally. You may also contact us to request deletion.
- **Portability:** Your tabs can be exported at any time from within the app, in several standard formats. The web app's Profile page also offers "Export all my tabs", which downloads your whole library as a JSON file.
- **Objection:** Object to certain processing activities.
- **Complaint:** Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **chewpacastudios@gmail.com**.
